# Varo Bank

> Varo Bank, N.A. is a mobile-first, FDIC-chartered digital bank offering fee-free checking, high-yield savings, early direct deposit, instant cash advances (Varo Advance), a secured credit-builder card (Varo Believe), and a personal line of credit. Varo does NOT operate a public first-party developer API or developer portal; consumer banking data is reachable by third-party applications only through US open-finance aggregators such as Plaid (a documented, native integration), and potentially MX, Finicity, Akoya, or Yodlee under customer-permissioned data access.

## Products

- [Varo Bank Account (checking)](https://www.varomoney.com/bank-account/): Fee-free checking, early direct deposit, debit card, 55,000+ Allpoint ATMs, cashback rewards.
- [Varo Savings Account](https://www.varomoney.com/savings-account/): High-yield savings up to 5.00% APY on balances up to $5,000, automated savings tools and goals.
- [Varo Advance (cash advance)](https://www.varomoney.com/cash-advance/): $20–$500 short-term advances, flat fee, no interest, auto-repaid on next direct deposit.
- [Varo Personal Line of Credit](https://www.varomoney.com/line-of-credit/): Up to $2,000, single flat fee, no interest, terms up to 12 months.
- [Varo Believe (credit builder)](https://www.varomoney.com/credit-builder/): Secured Visa credit-builder card reporting to all major bureaus, no annual fee, no interest.

## Data Access

- [Plaid integration (Varo customer story)](https://plaid.com/customer-stories/varo/): The real, documented seam through which third-party apps reach Varo account data (balances, transactions, identity, account/routing) under customer consent via Plaid's OAuth-style permissioned linking.
- [Open Banking Tracker profile](https://www.openbankingtracker.com/provider/varomoney): Third-party catalog of Varo's open-finance connectivity.

## Notes

- No first-party OpenAPI, GraphQL, or MCP endpoint is published. The GraphQL schema in this repo (graphql/varo-schema.graphql) is a CONCEPTUAL model of Varo's product data, not a live introspected API.
- api.varomoney.com is not a public developer host (returns HTTP 530).
- Status monitoring is via third-party StatusGator (https://statusgator.com/services/varo), not a Varo-operated status page.
