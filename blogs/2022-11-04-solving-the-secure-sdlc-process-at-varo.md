Application Security has dramatically evolved over the last 10 years. In the past, organizations responded reactively to identified security considerations based on their vulnerability and severity level triggering mitigation processes that could take hours, weeks, or even longer. Over time, proactive measures have become available to replace these risky reactive responses.
